Dear friends of Nepsis, God bless!

On 2 November, the church in Geneva had the blessing of welcoming His Eminence Metropolitan Iosif on a pastoral visit. His visit brought light, peace and joy to the hearts of the faithful, and the hierarchical Liturgy celebrated on this occasion was a moment of deep communion and spiritual emotion.

In his sermon, His Eminence urged the faithful to live their faith with an open heart and to show their love through works of mercy, recalling the parable of the rich man and Lazarus.

After the service, the young people of Nepsis and the other young people of the community had an open conversation with His Eminence and received answers full of wisdom, gentleness and fatherly love.

Thanks to the care of Father Adrian Diaconu and the involvement of the parishioners, the whole day unfolded in an atmosphere of celebration, joy and prayer, discovering the beauty of communion in Christ.
