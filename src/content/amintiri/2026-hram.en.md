On 28 February 2026 we celebrated the feast of the patron saint of Nepsis Switzerland, Saint John Cassian, at the Monastery “Protection of the Mother of God” in Les Sciernes d'Albeuve, with His Grace Theophilos of Iberia as our guest.

The announced programme included Matins, the Hierarchical Divine Liturgy, lunch at the monastery and a dialogue with Bishop Theophilos on the writings of Saint John Cassian.

You can listen to Bishop Theophilos’s sermon on [our YouTube channel](https://youtu.be/zag0ULcFwzY).
