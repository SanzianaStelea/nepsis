God bless 🙏! On 1 August we were able to meet Father Dionysius and organise the hike with catechesis in the Lauterbrunnen area.

📖 The catechesis was about the essence of Holy Communion, with the emphasis on our co-working in this mystery. Among the references used during the catechesis were the Gospel of Matthew, chapter 26, and Origen’s Commentary on the Gospel of Matthew, chapter 26.

🚶‍♀ We took an easy walk through the Lauterbrunnen valley, stopping at the Staubbach, Spissbach and Aegertenbach waterfalls and at the underground ⛰️ Trümmelbach falls.

A few moments are captured in the photos.
