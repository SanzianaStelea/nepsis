On Sunday, 5 October, with the help of God and of Saint John Cassian, we managed to bring together young people from different Swiss cantons in one place, as part of the project “Nepsis Switzerland: Time of Renewal”.

We attended the Divine Liturgy at the Romanian parish in Baden and then had lunch together.

The day continued with sessions presenting Nepsis, team building and brainstorming, where we shared ideas, plans and enthusiasm for the future.

New and long-standing Nepsis volunteers alike came with lots of energy and great suggestions for the next activities — we hope to put them all into practice!

It was a real joy to get to know each other (better) and to talk about the future of Nepsis!

We thank God, the Baden parish for its hospitality and organisation, and everyone who took part in this blessed new beginning!
